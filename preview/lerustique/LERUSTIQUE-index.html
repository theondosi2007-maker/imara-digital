The restaurant

Mediterranean, by the fire.

An intimate dining room with Moroccan warmth: an open fire, hardwood ceilings and the original flagstone floors. The cooking is refined, modern Mediterranean, made with organic and artisan ingredients, with a wine list from classic French châteaux to New World favourites.
