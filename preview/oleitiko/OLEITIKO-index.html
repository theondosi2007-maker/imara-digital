Karibu Ole Itiko

Wake up to the water hole.

Ole Itiko sits in Kinja Estate, between Hippo Point and Mundui, in a sheltered patch of acacia woodland. Bring the family, cook your own meals, light the fire in the evening and watch who comes down to drink.

  • Five self-catering cottages
  • Fully fitted kitchens
  • Fireplace in the family cottage
  • Pets welcome
  • Barbecue on request
