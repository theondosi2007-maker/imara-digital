Questions

What lodge owners ask us.

Do I have to leave Booking.com?

No. Keep every platform you are on. A direct booking page runs alongside them and catches the guests who look you up by name before booking — which is most of them. You pay commission on fewer nights, not on none.

How long does it take?

Five working days from the point we have your photos and room details. The photos are usually what decides the timeline.

What does it cost?

It depends on the size of your property and how many room types you need to show. Book a 15-minute call and we will give you a fixed price the same day — one number, in writing, with a delivery date.

What if I do not have good photos?

Send what you have. We will tell you honestly which rooms need reshooting and what to photograph — often a phone in good morning light is enough to start.

Can I update it myself later?

Yes. We show you how to change rates and text. For bigger changes, send a WhatsApp message and we will handle it.

Do you work with small lodges?

Most of our work is with owner-run properties. If you have four rooms, the commission you are paying still adds up to real money over a season.
